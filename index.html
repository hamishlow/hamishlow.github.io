Hamish Low

Senior Economist & Economic Advisor

Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago

✉ Email Scholar ORCID RePEc
  • Senior Economist and Economic Advisor at the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, and Research Fellow at the Institute for Fiscal Studies and CEPR. Previously James Meade Professor of Economics at Oxford (Nuffield College) and Professor of Economics at Cambridge (Trinity College).
  • My research asks what risks people face over their lives, and how far they can insure against them through saving, work, family and government programs. I combine life-cycle models with structural estimation, working across public finance, labor economics and macroeconomics.
  • Recent work covers disability insurance (a public lecture at Oxford), family formation and the safety net, and at the Chicago Fed, productivity and the labor market.
