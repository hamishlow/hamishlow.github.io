Why "The Stumble"?

Whether you're a seasoned runner or just finding your feet, we all stumble sometimes! This race celebrates the journey, the effort, and the bonds we strengthen along the way. The Wallace Monument watches over us as we stumble forward together!

🏆 Everyone's a Winner

This isn't just about who crosses the finish line first. It's about showing up, supporting each other, and creating memories that'll last a lifetime. (But we'll still keep track of times... just for bragging rights!)
