House Rules

💷 100 Stumble Pounds

Everyone starts with 100 Stumble Pounds (S£) to spread across the bets however they like. It is play money only: no real money changes hands.

🏦 Pool Betting

Each bet is a pool. When it is settled, everyone who backed the winner shares the whole pool in proportion to their stake. The odds move as people bet.

🔐 Name and PIN

Your first bet registers your name with a 4-digit PIN of your choice. Use the same PIN for later bets. If either runner doesn't start, that bet is void and stakes are refunded.
